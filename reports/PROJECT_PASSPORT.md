There are two types of inputs(the configs for the input are at all_configs.py):
1) Input to the model is DatasetCreationConfig.bg_shape(h=640, w=360) image that is loaded from video in the youtube sideman series that consists of a group called sideman playing among us. The images are then resized to TransformConfig.heght and TransformConfig.width.
2) Another input type is generated images with background taken randomly from maps folder. The figures are generated by the generate script.
